Privacy

How we handle your information.

McKinney Multifamily Group, RE/MAX Quinte Ltd., Brokerage.

What we collect. When you contact us, request a consultation, register as an investor, or subscribe to market updates, we collect what you give us: your name, email address, phone number, and any property or investment details you choose to provide.

What we use it for. To respond to you, to prepare for a conversation you have asked for, and — only if you subscribed — to send quarterly market intelligence. We do not sell, rent, or trade your information, and we do not share it outside the brokerage except where the law requires it or where a transaction you are party to requires it (for example, with your lawyer or lender at your direction).

Analytics. With your consent we use Google Analytics to understand how these pages are used. No advertising, no data resale. You can decline when you first visit, and change your choice at any time by clearing this site's data in your browser.

Retention. Records connected to a real estate transaction are kept as Ontario brokerage regulations require. Everything else is kept only as long as it is useful to respond to you, and deleted on request.

Your rights. You can ask what we hold about you, ask us to correct it, or ask us to delete it. Write to liam@mckinneyrealty.ca. You can unsubscribe from market updates with the link in any email.

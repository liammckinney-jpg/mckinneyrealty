The First Building · A Free Course

Eight lessons on the asset class nobody explains.

How apartment buildings are priced, financed, and compounded — delivered by email over roughly two weeks, with the honest parts left in. No cost, no obligation, unsubscribe anytime.

Starting out → The First Building, below. Already own, or evaluating a deal → The Mechanics, the reference library.

Most people are never shown how income real estate actually works. Not because it is complicated — the core of it fits on a napkin — but because the residential market has no reason to teach it. This course is the explanation we wish every first-time building buyer had received: the pricing model, the financing framework, the real costs, and the compounding mechanic, each in a five-to-eight-minute read.

It is written by a father-and-son brokerage team, and it is honest to a fault: one full lesson is about the taxes and tolls, another names the situations where buying a building is the wrong move, and the last one tells you plainly that most people finishing the course should not buy a building this month.

The compounding mechanic

Three mechanics, none complicated:

Tenants retire the debt — part of every rent cheque is mortgage principal, equity you never had to save. Income growth compounds into value — because buildings are priced on income, every dollar of new annual rent is roughly twenty dollars of building value (at a 5% cap rate). And refinancing recycles the equity — pull your capital back out against the grown value, without selling, and buy building two.

Repeat. That’s the entire strategy. No timing, no exit, no greater fool required.

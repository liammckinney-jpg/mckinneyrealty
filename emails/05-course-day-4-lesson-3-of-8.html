The First Building · Lesson 3 of 8
Reading a building like a buyer

Rent roll minus vacancy minus normalized expenses = NOI. NOI ÷ asking price = the cap rate the seller is really asking you to pay. That’s the entire literacy — two documents, five numbers, back of an envelope.

Lesson 3 teaches you to read both documents the way buyers do, including where sellers hide the soft spots: the suspiciously low maintenance line (deferred, not absent), the missing management fee, the 25% expense ratio that’s a story rather than a building.

Homework included: pull a real Ontario listing and price it yourself. First time takes twenty minutes. Tenth time takes four.

The First Building · Lesson 4 of 8
The financing nobody tells beginners about

Residential lending caps out — most A-lenders won’t hold more than four or five rental mortgages per borrower, because your salary can only stretch so far.

At five units, a property crosses into CMHC’s multi-family framework, and the question flips: not “can your income carry this debt?” but “can the building’s income carry this debt?” The building qualifies, not your T4. Insured rates run cheaper than conventional, amortizations run longer, and there’s no cap on how many buildings qualify themselves.

The reach math surprises people: a six-unit in a secondary market can require less equity than most assume — Lesson 4 works the illustration, plus the two cautions (process and premiums).

What working with a broker actually looks like

Six phases from decision to keys: defining what you’re buying, sourcing (including the off-market buildings that never hit a portal), underwriting, negotiation, due diligence, closing. Months, not weeks — a federal agency is involved, and thoroughness is the point.

What it costs: brokers earn commission on completed deals, and in most purchases it’s paid from the seller’s side. Talking costs nothing and obliges nothing.

And the closing note: most people finishing it shouldn’t buy a building this month. Keep your Modeler scenario alive. Read the Builder’s Guide when you want the deeper version. And when your numbers say it’s real — this year or in five — we’ll still be here.

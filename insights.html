2025 completions — the peak. Construction starts pausing as development economics break down.

Cap rates have stabilized after two years of expansion. The bid-ask gap that froze the market in 2023 has closed, and transaction volume surged 25.1% quarter-over-quarter in Q4 2025 as Bank of Canada rate cuts restored positive leverage.

Simultaneously, construction starts are pausing across the province. Punitive development charges, tariff-driven materials costs, and broken pro formas have shut down the new supply pipeline. National apartment completions peaked at 25,000 units in 2025 — the highest since 1987 — but that peak is now behind us.

These structural dynamics are converging with a generational ownership transition: aging operators facing refinancing at dramatically higher rates, six-figure capital items coming due on 1970s-era building systems, and insurance costs repriced to a permanently higher base over the past decade.

Common Questions

About McKinney Realty

Direct answers to the questions we are asked most often.

Who is McKinney Realty?

McKinney Realty is a father-and-son multi-family and investment real estate team serving all of Ontario. Liam McKinney (Broker, Property.ca Inc., Brokerage) and Sean McKinney (Broker of Record, RE/MAX Quinte Ltd., Brokerage) have completed more than $200M in combined career transaction volume, including more than $100M in multi-family, land, and commercial property.

What does McKinney Realty specialize in?

The sale and acquisition of apartment buildings and investment properties in the $2M–$10M range across Ontario, with particular depth in Ontario's secondary markets. The team advises both building owners on disposition and investors on acquisition, including CMHC multi-family financing pathways such as MLI Select.

Where does McKinney Realty operate?

Province-wide across Ontario. Liam McKinney is based in Toronto; Sean McKinney is based in the Quinte region of Eastern Ontario.

Does McKinney Realty work with first-time apartment building buyers?

Yes. The team publishes free public tools, including the Compounding Modeler at mckinneyrealty.ca/tools, and written guides covering underwriting, financing, and operations for investors moving into multi-family for the first time.

How is McKinney Realty compensated?

As licensed real estate brokers, through commission on completed transactions, under the brokerages named above. Consultations carry no fee or obligation.
